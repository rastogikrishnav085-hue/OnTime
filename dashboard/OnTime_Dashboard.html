What the model uses

  • Route distance and remaining delivery-time slack.
  • Weather severity and congestion forecasts.
  • Carrier reliability, facility backlog and dispatch capacity.
  • Route disruption alerts, tracking scan age and estimated truck delay.
  • Price, freight, product weight and purchase-to-dispatch lag.
Benchmark scope: operational signals and target labels in this dataset are simulated. The reported metrics describe the synthetic dispatch task only; they are not measured real-world delivery results or calibrated probabilities. Provide actual dispatch telemetry and validate against later labelled outcomes before relying on alerts.

Dataset and artifacts

  • Separate raw CSV: data/raw/synthetic_operational_orders.csv.
  • Portable trained model: models/synthetic_operational/xgboost_synthetic_operational.json.
  • Metrics and feature importance are saved beside the model.
  • The nine original Olist source CSVs remain separate and unchanged.
